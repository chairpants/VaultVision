## Source notes

Filenames are `1x01`-`1x12`, a complete gapless run matching the series'
actual 12 episodes — trusted as broadcast order. A small "animekat.tv"
watermark is burned into the picture's top-right corner throughout; that's
inherited from whatever rip this upload was sourced from, nothing this
project added or can remove.
