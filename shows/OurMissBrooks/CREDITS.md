## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/44555).

Files are named `Our Miss Brooks SxEyy Title`; titles agree with the
broadcast record apart from spelling. Missing from the item: S02E27 "The
Bakery", S02E31 "Just Remember the Red River Valley" and S03E02 "Blood,
Sweat and Laugh".
