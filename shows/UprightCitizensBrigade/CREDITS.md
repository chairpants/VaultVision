- [upright-citizens-brigade](https://archive.org/details/upright-citizens-brigade)

*Upright Citizens Brigade* is the property of its respective rights holders; this project
streams a publicly hosted recording and hosts no video content itself.

## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/6250).

Titles in the filenames are truncated ("Saigon Suicide", "Power Market") but
agree with the broadcast record. Missing from the item: S03E01 "Costumes".
