[Creative Commons Attribution](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Used as the player's TV set, embedded as glTF for web rendering.

## Video content

**The Internet Archive** — https://archive.org

This player streams one file from
[an archive.org item](https://archive.org/details/never-cry-wolf-1983)
(a multi-film collection item — only `The Breakfast Club (1985).mp4` is used here). Deep gratitude to the uploader. Support the Archive:
https://archive.org/donate

*The Breakfast Club* is the property of its respective rights holders; this project
streams a publicly hosted recording and hosts no video content itself.

## Poster art

`art/TheBreakfastClub.jpg` is the film's poster, via
[TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/movie/2108). Already a 2:3 portrait,
which is the shape the guide's card art box wants.
