## Video content

**The Internet Archive** — https://archive.org

[Item link](https://archive.org/details/GatchamanBotPG-ForceEagleRiders) —
*Eagle Riders* (1997), Saban's English-language re-edit of *Gatchaman II* and
*Gatchaman Fighter*, complete 65-episode run. This item also holds partial,
scattered episode sets of three related shows (the original *Science Ninja
Team Gatchaman*, its earlier English adaptation *Battle of the Planets*, and
*G-Force: Guardians of Space*) — those aren't added here since they're only a
handful of episodes each, nowhere near complete. Support the Archive:
https://archive.org/donate

*Eagle Riders* is the property of Saban Entertainment / Tatsunoko; this
project streams a publicly hosted recording and hosts no video content
itself.

## Source notes

Files are numbered `Episode 1`-`Episode 65`, a complete gapless run matching
the series' full episode count — trusted as broadcast order.

## Format note

None — ordinary single-season run, one episode per row.
