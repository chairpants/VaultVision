## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/8601).

Files are coded `SxxEyy` with episode numbers running straight through the
series (`S06E140`), so every file was pinned to its episode by title within
its season. Two moved: "Hide and Seek" is filed under season 2 but aired as
S03E08. TVmaze lists "Blue's School" and "Something to Do Blue" twice
(S04E20–21 and again S06E11–12); they appear once, in season 4.

Left out: an extended cut of "Blue's Birthday", the "Meet Joe Scrapbook
Adventure" special, *Blue's Big Musical Movie* and a *Blue's Clues & You*
item. Most rows point at the uploaded `.mp4` files (h.264/AAC, spot-checked
in three seasons).
