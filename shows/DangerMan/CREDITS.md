## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/16146).

The item has one folder per series (39 / 22 / 23 / 2 files, matching the
broadcast record) and numbers the files 01–86 straight through. Series 3's
numbering is the uploader's own and doesn't follow broadcast order, so every
file was pinned to its episode by title rather than by number. All 86 match.

Series 1 is the half-hour 1960–61 run; series 2–4 are the hour-long run, and
series 4 is the two colour episodes, *Koroshi* and *Shinda Shima*.

`Original US Opening.mp4` (the *Secret Agent* title sequence) is in the item
but isn't listed. Original `.mp4` uploads, h.264/AAC.
