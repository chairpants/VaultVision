streams a publicly hosted recording and hosts no video content itself.

## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/1135).

Files are named `The Time Tunnel S01Exx Title`; count and titles match the
broadcast record.

The item also holds `The Time Tunnel S02E00 - Pilot.mp4`. It's not a 1966
episode: frames from it show the unaired 2002 Fox remake pilot. It's listed in
season 0, labelled "2002 Remake Pilot" in the player.
