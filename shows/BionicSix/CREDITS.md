## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/4282).

Counts (22/43) and titles match the broadcast record. The library database
also grouped seven single-episode items here (`bionic-six-02` …) that
duplicate episodes already present; they're left out.
