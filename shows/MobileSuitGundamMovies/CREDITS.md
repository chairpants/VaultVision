## Video content

**The Internet Archive** — https://archive.org

[Item link](https://archive.org/details/gundammoviesdubbed) — the three 1981-82
theatrical compilation films that condense the original 1979 *Mobile Suit
Gundam* TV series (43 episodes) into a trilogy: *Mobile Suit Gundam I*,
*Mobile Suit Gundam II: Soldiers of Sorrow*, and *Mobile Suit Gundam III:
Encounters in Space*. English dub. Support the Archive:
https://archive.org/donate

*Mobile Suit Gundam* is the property of Sunrise/Sotsu; this project streams a
publicly hosted recording and hosts no video content itself.

## Source notes

The item ships each film as a `source: original` `.mp4` plus an Archive-derived
`.ia.mp4`; the derivative was used for all three. No episode-order judgment
calls here — three films, three parts, straightforward.

## Format note

`grouping: "flat"` — these are films, not episodes, so the guide reads "3
parts" and there are no season tabs.
