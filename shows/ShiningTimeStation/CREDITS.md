## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/8468).

Files are named `Shining Time Station - Ep. N - Title`, numbered straight
through; every file was pinned to its episode by title. TVmaze counts the
six "Mr. Conductor's Thomas Tales" (1996) as season 5, and those are included.
The 1990 holiday special "'Tis a Gift" is S01E21 (the TV airing; the item's
longer home-video cut is left out).

Not placed: "Ep. 47 - Billy's Party", which matches no listed title, and
S03E07 "Billy's Thanksgiving" has no file. They may be the same episode under
two names; that wasn't confirmed, so the slot stays empty.
