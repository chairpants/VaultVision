## Video content

**The Internet Archive** — https://archive.org

This player streams
[a single archive.org item](https://archive.org/details/Trucks_USA_Network_WOC_1997-10-29)
— an off-air recording with the original commercials intact (WOC). Real
off-air tape, not a clean rip, so expect the picture and the ad breaks that
come with that. Deep gratitude to the VHS-hoarder who kept it. Support the
Archive: https://archive.org/donate

*Trucks* is the property of its respective rights holders; this project
streams a publicly hosted recording and hosts no video content itself.

## Poster art

`art/Trucks.jpg` is the film's poster, via
[TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/movie/17100). Already a 2:3 portrait,
which is the shape the guide's card art box wants.
