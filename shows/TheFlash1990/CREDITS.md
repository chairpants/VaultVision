## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/528).

Files are named `The Flash S01Exx Title`; count and titles match the
broadcast record.

## Poster
