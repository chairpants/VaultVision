## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/19134).

Files are named `Exosquad - SxxEyy - Title`; counts (13/39) and titles match
the broadcast record. TVmaze adds each episode's story-arc name to season 1
titles ("Pirate Scourge (Fall of the Human Empire 1)"); those are kept.
