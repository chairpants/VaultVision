## Video content

**The Internet Archive** — https://archive.org

[lily-c.-a.-t](https://archive.org/details/lily-c.-a.-t) — a MakeMKV rip of
Discotek Media's DVD release of the 1987 anime film. The item carries both
the original MKV rip and Archive's own h.264/AAC derivative `.mp4`; the
derivative was used here.

Support the Archive: https://archive.org/donate

*Lily C.A.T.* is the property of its respective rights holders; this project
streams a publicly hosted recording and hosts no video content itself.

## Source notes

Single audio track (`und` — no language tag in the file itself), but the
opening credits are burned into the picture in English (`Music Composer
AKIRA INOUE`, etc.), which is consistent with this being the English dub
audio rather than a Japanese-only disc. Not independently confirmed by ear.
Runtime (~67 min) matches the film's known length.
