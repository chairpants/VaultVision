## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/16893).

Files are named `Perversions.of.Science.S01Exx_Title_NTSC.DVD`; count and
titles match the broadcast record.
