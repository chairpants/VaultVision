## Source notes

Seasons 3-8 aren't on the Archive as far as this search found, so the guide
stops after season 2 rather than thinning out across the rest of the run.

Both items are named after whichever episode the uploader happened to finish
last — season 1 is `legacy-…` and season 2 is
`15-my-brothers-back-and-theres-going-to-be-trouble-…`. The ids say nothing
about what's in them; the item titles ("wings season 1", "Wings Season 2") are
the reliable part.

Episode titles come from Wikipedia's episode list, not the filenames. The
filenames are lowercase, drop punctuation, and carry the uploader's own
timestamps — "10 there always room for cello_20260729233942638.mp4" for
"There's Always Room for Cello". Their leading numbers do happen to match
broadcast order here, but they were matched by title rather than trusted, and
each match was scored so a mismatch would have been visible.
