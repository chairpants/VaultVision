## Source notes

**S01E01, "War Zone", is missing from the item.** The player therefore starts
at S01E02, "The Long Road". Everything else is present: 12 of the 13
produced episodes.

The uploader's files carry both their own chronological number (`116`–`127`)
and a `1xEE` code. Those `1xEE` codes match TNT's **broadcast** order exactly,
which is the order used here. Note that *Crusade*'s production order differs
from the broadcast order, and JMS's preferred order differs from both — if an
episode feels out of sequence, that is the show, not the upload.

Sources are `.mkv` originals; the rows point at their same-stem `.mp4` h.264
derivatives. The item also holds stale `.mp4` derivatives from an earlier
renumbering; those are ignored.
