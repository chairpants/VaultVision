## Source notes

The uploader's own numbering (`01`-`17`) is **not** broadcast order — checked
every title against Wikipedia's episode list and found two places where it
disagrees: files `04`/`05` are swapped (`Honest Abe and Popular Steve` is
really episode 5, `Wrestling with an Angel` is episode 4), and files
`10`/`12`/`14`/`15`/`17` are shuffled relative to their real episodes 10,
11-14, 15. The guide lists all 17 under their real `S01Exx` code and title;
`fileHint` still points at the file's actual (mislabeled-number) filename.
Also excluded: several TGIF bumpers and promos bundled in the same item —
not episodes.
