## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/727).

Files are numbered straight through by DVD volume and disc (`Pee-Wee Vol 1
Disc 1 E03 Raint day`), so every file was pinned to its episode by title
(typos included). The *Christmas Special* sits in season 0. The item's 1981
HBO stand-up special is a different show and is left out.
