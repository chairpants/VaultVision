All 30 episodes (ITV 1968–69; NBC in the US), in one item:

- [the-champions-complete-series-1967](https://archive.org/details/the-champions-complete-series-1967)

Thanks to the uploader. Support the Archive: https://archive.org/donate

*The Champions* is the property of its respective rights holders; this project
streams a publicly hosted recording and hosts no video content itself.

## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/11330).

Files are named `The Champions   Episode NN   Title DivX`; the 30 files and
their titles match the broadcast record in order. The rows point at the
archive's h.264 derivatives, not the DivX uploads.
