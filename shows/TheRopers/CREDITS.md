## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/22138).

Files are named `The Ropers SxxEyy Title`; titles agree with the broadcast
record. Missing: S02E01 "The Party".

The library database grouped two other items under this show, both left out:
a sitcom podcast episode about the series (`ropers-final`), and a second
upload of two season 2 episodes, one of them mislabelled "Man About the
House".
