## Source notes

Files are numbered `01`-`49` inside a nested folder with no gaps or
duplicates, matching the broadcast episode count exactly — trusted as
broadcast order. Two title-card typos survive in the filenames themselves
(`06Party Night_.ia.mp4`, `43Target.. Earth.ia.mp4`); the guide shows the
corrected titles ("Party Night", "Target: Earth") while the `fileHint` still
points at the actual, typo'd filename. Two episodes (23, 32) use the dub's
"God of Death" phrasing for Duo's nickname where some episode-list sources
say "the Great Destroyer" — both are attested translations of the same
Japanese title; kept what the dub itself uses.
