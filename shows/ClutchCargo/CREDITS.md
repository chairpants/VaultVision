## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/28248).

Count and titles match the broadcast record. The library database also
grouped seven single-episode items here that duplicate episodes already
present; they're left out.
