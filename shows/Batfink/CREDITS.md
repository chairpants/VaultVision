## Video content

All 100 episodes (syndicated, 1966–67), in one item:

- [batfink-the-complete-collection-1966-67](https://archive.org/details/batfink-the-complete-collection-1966-67)

Thanks to the uploader. Support the Archive: https://archive.org/donate

*Batfink* is the property of its respective rights holders; this project
streams a publicly hosted recording and hosts no video content itself.

## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/13142).

Count and titles match the broadcast record.

## Poster

the guide card and the tall art.
