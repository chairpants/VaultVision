## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/8364).

From "The Complete Collection". Files are named `SxxEyy Title`; per-season
counts match the broadcast record (the 1979 local season's titles differ in
wording; TVmaze's are used). Where an episode exists in two cuts, the
"Original Broadcast" / "Uncut Original Broadcast" version is used; the
edited rerun and CTV cuts are left out. S09E12 "Punishment" is incomplete in
the source.

Season 0 ("Specials & Bloopers" in the player) holds *The Worst of You
Can't Do That on Television*, the *UFO Kidnapped* special, *You Can't Do That
on Film* and three blooper reels. Left out: *Whatever Turns You On* and its
backstage reel, the five "DLN" episodes and "Project 131", which are other
productions.
