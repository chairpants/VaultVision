## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/8962).

The 1997 live-action series (the 1992 cartoon is its own show here).

Episode codes and titles otherwise come from the files' own numbering, checked against the broadcast record; where filenames carry no titles they're labelled by position, an educated guess rather than a frame-by-frame check.
