## Source notes

Filenames already carry `SxxEyy` codes and titles matching Wikipedia's
episode list exactly, in order — no renumbering needed. Each episode ships as
both a `source: original` `.mkv` and a `derivative` `.mp4`; the derivative was
used throughout.

## Format note

None — ordinary single-season series, one episode per row.
