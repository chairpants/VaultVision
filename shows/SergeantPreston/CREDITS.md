## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/60947).

Files are named `Sergeant Preston of the Yukon SxxEyy Title`; per-season
counts (33/23/22) match the broadcast record and the titles agree apart from
spelling ("Go Fever" for "Gold Fever", "Ice Pack Justice" for "Pack Ice
Justice"). Broadcast titles are used. 720p.
