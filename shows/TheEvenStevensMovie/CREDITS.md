## Video content

**The Internet Archive** — https://archive.org

This player streams one file from
[an archive.org item](https://archive.org/details/freaky-friday-2003_202307)
(a multi-film collection item — only `The Even Stevens Movie (2003).mp4` is used here). Deep gratitude to the uploader. Support the Archive:
https://archive.org/donate

*The Even Stevens Movie* is the property of its respective rights holders; this project
streams a publicly hosted recording and hosts no video content itself.

## Poster art

`art/TheEvenStevensMovie.jpg` is a frame grabbed from the source file itself (the
Archive's own generated thumbnail), not official key art.
