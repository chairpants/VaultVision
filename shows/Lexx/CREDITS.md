## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/1656).

Files are named `Lexx - 0Nx0N - Title` (season 1's first movie is
`LEXX_SEASON1_1-Episode-1`); per-season counts (4/20/13/24) match the
broadcast record and titles agree. Rows point at h.264 `.ia.mp4` derivatives.
Lexx is an adult series (nudity, violence).
