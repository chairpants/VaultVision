## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/3184).

One folder per season, files named `Alias Smith and Jones - 1x01 - Title`;
counts (15/23/12) and titles match the broadcast record. The uploaded files
are MPEG-4 Part 2 (DivX-style) video that browsers can't decode, so every row
points at the archive's h.264 `.ia.mp4` derivative.
