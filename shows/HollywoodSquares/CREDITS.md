## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/2126).

628 dated broadcasts from 1967 to 2004 across several runs (NBC daytime, the 1986 revival, the 1998 Bergeron era). Each year is a "season" (`S1987E001` …), in air-date order, and each title ends with the run in brackets. A smaller second item and seven 9/11-week WJLA recordings grouped under this show are left out.

Episode codes and titles otherwise come from the files' own numbering, checked against the broadcast record; where filenames carry no titles they're labelled by position, an educated guess rather than a frame-by-frame check.
