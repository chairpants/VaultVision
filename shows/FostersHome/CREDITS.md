## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/7335).

Files are named by code only, with no titles. Per-season counts match the
broadcast record, so per `ADDING_A_SHOW.md` §1c the numbering is trusted and
titles come from the episode list by position. That is an educated guess, not
a frame-by-frame check.

Seasons 1–5 are complete (13/13/14/13/13). Season 6 has 10 of 13; missing
from the item: S06E11 "Read 'Em and Weep", S06E12 "Fools and Regulations", S06E13 "Goodbye to Bloo".
