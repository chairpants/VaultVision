## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/9475).

Files are named `Annie Oakley sxxeyy-TITLE [360p]`; per-season counts
(26/20/35) match the broadcast record and the titles agree (three are
shortened, e.g. "Santa Wears a Gun" for "Santa Claus Wears a Gun"). 360p.
