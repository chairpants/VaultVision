## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/5914).

Files are named `As Told By Ginger Season N_SxxEyy_Title`; every file was
pinned to its episode by title. Two three-part stories are single
movie-length files, listed as `S01E16-18` "Summer at Camp Caprice" and
`S03E18-20` "The Wedding Frame". Season 3's opening three-parter is filed
under its alternate title "Foutleys on Ice" and is TVmaze's "Far from Home".
