## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/19136).

The uploader's three season folders (5 / 8 / 8) don't match the broadcast
split (13 / 8: the five-part "Dark Water" opener plus eight more, then eight),
so every file was pinned to its episode by title. All 21 match.
