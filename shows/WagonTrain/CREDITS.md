## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/8420).

Files are named `Wagon Train SxxEyy Title`, and per-season counts match the
broadcast record, but a handful sit in the wrong slot, so every file was
pinned to its episode by title. That moved four: season 1's two-part "The
Major Adams Story" and "The Charles Maury Story" (filed as E31/E32/E30, aired
E30/E31/E32), and season 4's "The Don Alvarado Story" (filed E39; the season
has 38).

One stays uncertain: the file in S03E24 is titled "The Amos Gibbon Story",
which matches no episode in the record. It sits in the slot of "The Christine
Elliott Story" and is labelled that.

Left out: two John Wayne feature films filed in season 1 (*The Big Trail*,
*Wagon Master*), and three single-episode `.ogv` items that duplicate
episodes already present.
