## Source notes

Found via the AutoArchive library database (`library-db/`, export of
2026-11-12). Episode numbering and titles were checked against TVmaze's
episode list (https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/33871).

Files are named `Ozzy & Drix Sx Exx - Title`; counts (13/13) and titles match
the broadcast record. Rows point at h.264 `.ia.mp4` derivatives.
